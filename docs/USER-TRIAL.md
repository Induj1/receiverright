# A short receiving trial

**Status: protocol prepared; no participant results have been collected.** This is a formative usability check, not proof of savings or market demand. Do not fill the results with guesses.

Ask one shopkeeper who receives packaged goods and one supplier or delivery representative to try ReceiveRight. They may participate together or separately. Explain that it is a hackathon prototype and ask permission to take anonymized notes. Use a synthetic invoice if they do not want their own document processed. Do not upload a real invoice without permission; remove private customer/payment details first.

## Before showing the app (3 minutes)

Ask and write down their actual answers:

1. Tell us about the most recent delivery with a missing, damaged, or wrong item. What happened next?
2. How did you record and send the discrepancy? What was difficult about that process?
3. How often has this happened in the last month? Record “does not know” when appropriate.
4. Who responds on the supplier side, and what information do they need?

Do not start by telling them the app will save time or money. A person who has never encountered the problem is a useful finding.

## Receiver task (5 minutes)

Open https://ds687e8jj0.execute-api.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com on the device they normally use. Give them the task below without guiding every click:

> Record this delivery, check the invoice fields, enter what arrived, and prepare the information you would send to the supplier.

Use a delivery they understand or the clearly labeled sample. Record start/end times, any corrections to extraction, points of confusion, requests for help, and whether they finish. If you assist, record exactly where; do not count that as unassisted completion.

## Supplier task (3 minutes)

Open the review link on a separate device and provide its PIN separately. Ask:

> Review the delivery discrepancy and respond as you would during your normal work.

Record whether they can find the relevant invoice, count, and evidence, and whether they understand acknowledgement versus refund. Do not ask them to acknowledge facts they disagree with.

## After the task (3 minutes)

- What, if anything, was better than your current method?
- What would prevent you from using this on your next delivery?
- Would you actually open this link when another shop/supplier sent it? Why?
- What would need to change before you could use it?

Prefer their exact words, with permission, over a leading “Would you recommend it?” question.
